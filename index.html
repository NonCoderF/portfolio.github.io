My journey into software engineering began with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, but my passion for technology led me to teach myself Java, Data Structures & Algorithms, and Kotlin. That curiosity evolved into a successful career as an Android Engineer, where I've spent over six years building scalable, production-ready mobile applications used by thousands of users.

Throughout my career, I've modernized legacy codebases by migrating applications from MVP to MVVM, adopted Jetpack Compose and Material 3, built reusable Android libraries and Gradle plugins, and developed robust networking solutions using Retrofit, OkHttp, and Apollo GraphQL. Beyond professional work, I enjoy writing technical articles, contributing to open source, and creating side projects such as ArchGuard, TaporiAI, and Sally Launcher.
