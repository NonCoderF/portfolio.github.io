# Career Timeline And Journey

Purpose: Single source of truth for career progression, career narrative, and high-level professional journey. Company-specific details live in experience memory.

Career timeline:
Mechanical Engineering -> curiosity about programming -> Java -> Data Structures and Algorithms -> Android -> Kotlin -> early Android roles at KBG Software and Geekworkx Technologies -> Senior Android Engineer at Vantage Circle from July 2020 to 8 May 2026 -> architecture modernization -> Jetpack Compose -> Material3 -> modularization -> performance work -> sensor and ML-related Android work -> SDKs -> Gradle plugins -> AI products -> developer platforms and future engineering systems.

Career journey:
I began from Mechanical Engineering and moved into software by teaching myself Java. I learned Data Structures and Algorithms because I wanted the problem-solving foundation behind programming, not just syntax. Android became my main engineering direction because it combined the parts of software I enjoy: product thinking, UI, architecture, performance, platform APIs, and real user impact.

Professionally, I grew through KBG Software and Geekworkx Technologies before joining Vantage Circle. I worked at Vantage Circle as a Senior Android Engineer until 8 May 2026. A major part of that role was moving a large legacy codebase toward a more modern architecture: MVP to MVVM, Compose adoption, Material3, modularization, performance optimization, step detection improvement, ML Kit Pose Detection, mentoring, and architecture decisions.

Outside company work, I kept building products and tools. ArchGuard reflects my interest in architecture automation. SonicBridge reflects my interest in Android platform APIs, local networking, and real-time audio. Tapori AI reflects my interest in AI products. Biometric SDK reflects my interest in reusable SDKs. Sally Launcher reflects my interest in deeper Android platform experiences. Shockwave is a future vision around affordable immersive entertainment, audio, acoustic engineering, AI room planning, and product design.
